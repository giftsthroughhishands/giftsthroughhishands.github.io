Our mission
Gifts Through His Hands is a nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing hope, love, and Christmas joy to children and families in need. Through the generosity of donors and volunteers, we work together to make sure more children experience the magic of Christmas.

We are a volunteer-run nonprofit in our third year. We keep no funds. Every dollar goes to gifts for children.

How it works

One day. One community. Every gift.

On December 11 we come together in Rexburg to do it all as a group.

  1. We shop together

    Volunteers use every donated dollar to buy gifts picked for each child.

  2. We wrap together

    Friends, families and neighbors gather to wrap every present by hand.

  3. We deliver together

    Teams deliver the gifts to families so children have presents for Christmas.

100% goes to the kids.We keep nothing. Every donation buys gifts for children in need.
