Our mission
Gifts Through His Hands is a nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing hope, love, and Christmas joy to children and families in need. Through the generosity of donors and volunteers, we work together to make sure more children experience the magic of Christmas.

We're a volunteer-run nonprofit in our third year, and everything donated goes to gifts for kids.

How it works

It all happens in one night

On December 11, volunteers meet in Rexburg and get it all done in one evening.

  1. We shop together

    Volunteers take the donations and buy gifts picked out for each child.

  2. We wrap together

    Friends, families and neighbors gather to wrap every present by hand.

  3. We deliver together

    Teams drop the gifts off with each family in time for Christmas.

100% goes to the kids.We don't keep a dime. Your donation goes toward gifts for kids in need.
